Latest Project

Smart energy optimizer for buildings
This kind of project uses IoT sensors and machine learning to monitor how energy is used in offices or apartment buildings. It tracks temperature, occupancy, and electricity demand in real time, then automatically adjusts heating, cooling, and lighting. The goal is to cut energy waste without people noticing any drop in comfort. What makes it compelling is that the impact is immediate and measurable: lower bills and a smaller carbon footprint.
